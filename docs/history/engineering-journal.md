🏛️ Engineering Decision #003

Title: Repository-First Engineering

Status: ✅ Approved

Decision

Miltek Labs will establish a scalable repository structure before significant application development begins.

Rationale

A well-designed repository:

Improves onboarding.
Reduces technical debt.
Creates consistent engineering practices.
Makes documentation discoverable.
Scales with the organization.

Expected Outcome

Engineers spend less time asking where things belong and more time delivering value.

## Engineering Model v0.0.1 Approved — August 13, 2026

Engineering Model v0.0.1 was reviewed and approved.

The model establishes the foundational domain concepts for MEIP across
business and customer context, product and planning, requirements,
development, validation, environments and deployments, engineering
evidence, risk, release governance, people and ownership.

The model also establishes Engineering Lineage, Change Intelligence,
provenance, versioning, auditability, and modular adoption as foundational
cross-cutting concepts.

Key architectural principle:

MEIP will model the connected engineering lifecycle rather than organizing
its core domain around individual external tools or vendors.

The model is intentionally evolutionary. Future changes will be evaluated
through architecture review and Architecture Decision Records where
appropriate.
